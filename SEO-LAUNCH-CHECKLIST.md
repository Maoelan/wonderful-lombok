# Wonderful Lombok SEO Launch Checklist

Search visibility is a long-term outcome, not a guaranteed ranking position. The application now provides the technical foundation. Complete the account and authority tasks below when the client-owned domain is ready.

## Before connecting the final domain

- Choose one permanent HTTPS hostname, either the apex domain or `www`, and redirect the other hostname to it.
- Set `PUBLIC_SITE_URL` in production to the final canonical origin, without a trailing slash.
- Configure a permanent redirect from the current Vercel preview hostname to the final domain after approval.
- Confirm that every public page returns the expected canonical URL, localized `hreflang` links, and an indexable robots directive.
- Confirm that `/robots.txt` references the final-domain sitemap and that `/sitemap.xml` contains only canonical public URLs.
- Keep `/admin`, `/login`, and `/api/` excluded from indexing.

## Search-engine accounts

- Verify the final domain in Google Search Console using a DNS record owned by the client.
- Submit `https://FINAL-DOMAIN/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console.
- Verify the final domain in Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the same sitemap.
- Inspect the homepage, tour catalog, and at least one package URL in Search Console after deployment.
- Store verification credentials only in the hosting or DNS provider. Never commit them to this repository.

## Local search

- Create or update the Google Business Profile with the exact business name, phone number, final website, service area, and real business category.
- Use the same business name and contact details across the website, social profiles, maps, accommodation partners, and reputable travel directories.
- Add genuine client photographs and request honest reviews after completed trips. Do not purchase or fabricate reviews.

## Content and authority

- Give each package page one clear search intent based on its actual itinerary and destinations.
- Add original destination guidance only when the client can provide accurate local information.
- Link relevant destinations, tour packages, and transfer services to each other using descriptive link text.
- Seek legitimate references from hotels, homestays, tourism partners, local publications, and travel directories.
- Do not buy links, publish doorway pages, hide keywords, or mass-generate thin destination pages.

## Measurement after launch

- Record indexed-page count, organic impressions, clicks, average position, click-through rate, and booking conversions after the final domain is indexed.
- Review performance by landing page, query, language, country, and device each month.
- Improve pages that receive impressions but have weak click-through rates by clarifying titles and descriptions without keyword stuffing.
- Improve pages that receive traffic but few bookings by checking service clarity, price context, and the WhatsApp booking path.
- Revalidate structured data and Core Web Vitals after every material template or image change.

## Expected timing

Discovery and re-indexing can take days or weeks after a domain launch. Competitive first-page rankings usually require sustained content quality, real local authority, and user engagement in addition to correct technical SEO.
